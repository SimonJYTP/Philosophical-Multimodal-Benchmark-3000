# Problem

Build a reproducible philosophical multimodal benchmark with exactly 3,000 records, more than 80% genuine local-image coverage, balanced evaluation strata, blind-test inputs without answer leakage, and traceable source provenance.

The main unresolved research gaps are expert review and inter-annotator agreement for the HL enrichment tier, empirical model baselines, and explicit redistribution permission for HSSBench and MM-MoralBench images.
